How it works

1

Choose your productive and unproductive apps

Pick the apps and websites you work in, and the ones that eat your time. Everything else is neutral and doesn't count either way. On your Mac, describe what you work on and Tythe judges websites for you.

2

Productive work earns credits

Every minute in a productive app or website earns credits, on your Mac or your iPhone. You choose the rate, from 3 minutes of work for 1 credit up to 1 minute of work for 3 credits. Exercise from Apple Health can earn credits too.

3

Unproductive apps spend them

Social, video and games use your credits up, one credit per minute, on either device. Your Mac and iPhone share one balance, so credits earned on one can be spent on the other.

4

Run out, and unproductive apps lock

Until you do more productive work. On iPhone, Screen Time locks them. On Mac, a frosted lock screen covers them, even full-screen games. Earn more credits and they unlock on their own.

Questions

Do I need both a Mac and an iPhone?

No. Tythe works on an iPhone on its own, a Mac on its own, or both together. With both, they share one balance.

Is it free?

Yes, the beta is free to join through TestFlight. The first 100 beta testers also keep Tythe free for life. After that, paid plans will start at $4 a month, $40 a year, or $100 once for lifetime access.

Who decides what's productive?

You do. You choose your productive and unproductive apps. On your Mac you can also describe your work, add websites, and correct any page Tythe gets wrong so it learns.

What happens when I run out of credits?

Your unproductive apps lock. On iPhone you'll see a Screen Time lock screen; on Mac a frosted lock screen covers the app. Do more productive work and they unlock automatically. Your productive apps always stay open.

Which browsers work on Mac?

Safari and Chrome.

What happens to my data?

Your activity stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud, which we can't see. Tythe can send a short anonymous daily summary to help improve the app, with no app or website names, times or health data. You can switch it off in Settings. Read the privacy policy.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or a Mac with macOS 14 or later. Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on to share a balance.
