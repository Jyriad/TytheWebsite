Earn your screen time, across Mac and iPhone.

How it works

  • Time spent on productive apps and websites earns you time.
  • Exercise can earn you time too.
  • Unproductive apps and websites spend that time. When it runs out, they pause until you earn more.
  • Your balance stays in step between your Mac and iPhone through your own iCloud.
