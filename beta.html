What you should know first

What TestFlight is

TestFlight is Apple's official app for trying apps before they reach the App Store. You install TestFlight once, then use it to install and update Tythe safely, through Apple.

Why Tythe is on TestFlight

Tythe is still in beta. TestFlight lets a small group try the real Mac and iPhone apps, send feedback, and help shape what ships. It is not a website demo or a third-party download.

What you get

Full access to Tythe on Mac, iPhone, or both. The first 100 beta testers keep Tythe free for life. Setup takes a couple of minutes, with no signup beyond your Apple Account.

How to join

  1. Join Tythe on TestFlight Open the public invite link on your iPhone or Mac. That link is your invite — you do not need a separate redeem code.
  2. Don't have TestFlight yet? Install it free from the App Store, then come back to the invite link. Get TestFlight
  3. Install Tythe and finish setup Tap Install, open Tythe, and follow the short setup. Using both devices? Do the same on the other one.
